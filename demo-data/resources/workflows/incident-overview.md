# Incident Overview Template

Use this template when the agent needs to produce the current executive overview and the last-24-hours briefing slide for an incident.

## Inputs

- latest overview, timeline, and artifacts tabs
- last 24 hours of major actions, detections, and customer impact
- open risks, blockers, and next actions

## Output sections

- one-paragraph executive summary
- current status and business impact
- top three risks
- next three actions

## Slide prompt

Generate two concise status-call slides in markdown-friendly structure:

1. `Overview slide`
   - incident title
   - current severity and status
   - one-sentence business impact
   - top risks and next actions

2. `Last 24 hours slide`
   - key events by time
   - decisions made
   - newly discovered risks
   - actions scheduled for the next shift
